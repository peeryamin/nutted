1. What BugSeek AI is

BugSeek AI ("the Service") is a browser extension and companion backend API that assists with authorized security testing: passive reconnaissance of web pages, and — only with your explicit confirmation — guided active security testing of targets you are authorized to test.

BugSeek AI is a tool, not an authorization. It does not grant you permission to test anything. It does not determine whether your testing is legal.

2. Authorized testing only

You may use active testing features only against targets you are authorized to test, meaning at least one of:

  • (a) a target you own or operate;
  • (b) a target explicitly in scope of a bug bounty program whose rules you have read and accepted;
  • (c) a target covered by a written penetration-testing contract or engagement letter naming you (or your employer) as the tester;
  • (d) an intentionally vulnerable training application (e.g., OWASP Juice Shop, DVWA, HackTheBox) used in accordance with its own terms.

Passive reconnaissance (analyzing content your browser already loads) is lower-risk, but you remain responsible for complying with the target's terms of service and applicable law.

Before any active test run, the Service requires you to explicitly confirm authorization and define the scope. This confirmation is stored with the scan's authorization record (target, scope, timestamp). Misrepresenting authorization is a material breach of these Terms.

3. Scope enforcement

You must define the in-scope hosts, domains, and endpoints before active testing. The Service will refuse to test anything outside the scope you define. You must not attempt to bypass, disable, or misrepresent scope enforcement.

4. Acceptable use — you must NOT

  • Test any system without authorization (§2).
  • Use the Service to cause, or risk causing, denial of service or degradation of any system. Active testing is rate-limited by design; you must not attempt to circumvent rate limits.
  • Exfiltrate, store, or redistribute sensitive data encountered during testing (passwords, session tokens, personal data, financial data, private communications). Findings should describe the vulnerability, not reproduce the sensitive data.
  • Use the Service to develop or deliver malware, or to facilitate wrongdoing by others.
  • Submit reports generated by the Service to bounty platforms in bulk, via automation, or in violation of a platform's rules. BugSeek AI formats reports; a human must review and submit each one.
  • Reverse-engineer the backend API to evade metering, reproduce the proprietary agent, or resell Service output as a competing service (beyond what the open-source client license permits).

5. Your liability

You are solely responsible and liable for your testing activities. You agree to indemnify and hold harmless the operator against claims arising from your use of the Service, including unauthorized testing, scope violations, and data mishandling.

6. Data handling

  • Passive recon runs locally in the extension.
  • Guided agent scans transmit the page context required for testing to our backend API over TLS. We do not intentionally collect credentials, cookie secret values, or personal data; if such data appears in scan context, it is redacted or discarded per our data-handling pipeline and never used to train models without opt-in consent.
  • Anonymized, aggregated scan telemetry may be used to improve detection models only with your opt-in.
  • We will never sell scan data.
